PAN PACIFIC DEFENSE CORPS

UNITED IN THE DRIFT

Founded in the wake of the first Kaiju attack in 2013, the Pan Pacific Defense Corps unified 35 nations under one mission: build mechanical giants capable of matching a Kaiju blow for blow. The answer was the Jaeger — a 2,000-ton weapon of war controlled by two pilots fused in a shared neural link called the Drift.

Through the Drift, two minds become one — sharing memories, instincts, and reflexes at the speed of thought. The neural load is too great for one person alone. Together, humanity found a way to fight back.
